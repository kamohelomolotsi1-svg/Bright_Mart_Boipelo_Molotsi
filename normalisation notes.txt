Creating dim tables

*CustomerSince in DimCustomer, not DimDate.
* What belongs in DimDate?

A Date dimension is used for business events that you want to analyze over time.

Examples:

Transaction Date
Order Date
Ship Date
Delivery Date
Invoice Date

These are dates that occur on facts (transactions).

For example:

TransactionID	DateKey	CustomerKey	Amount
T1001	20260701	101	250.00

The DateKey points to DimDate, allowing you to answer questions like:

Sales by Month
Sales by Quarter
Sales by Year
What is CustomerSince?

CustomerSince is an attribute describing the customer.

It answers:

"When did this customer become a customer?"

That information belongs to the customer itself, just like:

FirstName
LastName
LoyaltyTier
Email

For example:

DimCustomer

CustomerKey	Name	LoyaltyTier	CustomerSince
101	John Smith	Gold	2021-03-15

If John makes 100 purchases, you don't want to join to DimDate just to know when he first became a customer.
